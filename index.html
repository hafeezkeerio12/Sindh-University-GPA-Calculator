How Quality Points Are Calculated

Quality Points are calculated by multiplying Credit Hours by Grade Point.

Quality Points = Credit Hours × Grade Point

Examples:

A+ → 4.00 × 3 CH = 12.00 QP
A+ → 4.00 × 2 CH = 8.00 QP
B+ → 3.20 × 3 CH = 9.60 QP
